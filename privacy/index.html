Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

ClipPin is a simple macOS menu bar app for saving and copying frequently used plain text snippets. This Privacy Policy explains how ClipPin handles information.

Summary

  • ClipPin does not collect personal data.
  • ClipPin does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
  • ClipPin does not require an account.
  • ClipPin does not use cloud sync.
  • ClipPin does not access the network.
  • Text snippets are stored locally on your Mac.

Data Collection

ClipPin does not collect, transmit, sell, share, analyze, or otherwise process personal data.

Local Storage

Text snippets added to ClipPin are stored locally on your Mac using the app’s local storage. They are not uploaded to any server and are not shared with the developer or any third party.

Clipboard Access

ClipPin writes a selected snippet to the macOS clipboard when you choose to copy it. ClipPin does not read your current clipboard and does not manage clipboard history.

Network Access

ClipPin does not require network access. The app does not connect to external servers, does not download remote content, and does not upload your snippets.

Accounts and Cloud Sync

ClipPin does not require an account and does not provide cloud sync.

Analytics and Advertising

ClipPin does not include analytics, tracking, advertising SDKs, or third-party data collection tools.

Children’s Privacy

ClipPin does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if ClipPin’s functionality changes. The latest version will be available on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, support, or bug reports, contact: loulan@loulan.me
