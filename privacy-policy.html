Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

ClipPin is a simple macOS menu bar app for saving and copying frequently used plain text snippets. This Privacy Policy explains how ClipPin handles information.

Data Collection

ClipPin does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or analyze any personal data.

Local Storage

Text snippets added to ClipPin are stored locally on your Mac. They are not uploaded to any server.

Clipboard Access

ClipPin writes selected snippets to the macOS clipboard when you choose to copy them. ClipPin does not read your current clipboard and does not manage clipboard history.

Network Access

ClipPin does not require an account, does not use cloud sync, and does not access the network.

Analytics and Advertising

ClipPin does not include analytics, tracking, advertising SDKs, or third-party data collection tools.

Third-Party Services

ClipPin does not send data to third-party services.

Children’s Privacy

ClipPin does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if ClipPin’s functionality changes. The latest version will be available on this page.

Contact
